Google Workspace + Microsoft 365 + marketing tools: staying under the SPF lookup limit

Most domains hit the 10-lookup limit the same way: one mailbox provider, then a few SaaS tools that each add an include. Measured with the free checker on 26 November 2026, here is what some common includes cost:

  • include:_spf.google.com (Google Workspace): 1
  • include:spf.protection.outlook.com (Microsoft 365): 1
  • include:sendgrid.net: 2 (it nests another include)
  • include:servers.mcsv.net (Mailchimp): 1
  • include:mail.zendesk.com: 1
  • include:_spf.salesforce.com: 2 (includes an exists: macro, which can't be flattened)

Provider records change, so run the checker against your own domain for current numbers.

Patterns that stay under 10

  • Only list what sends with your domain in the envelope sender (Return-Path). Many tools send with their own Return-Path and authenticate with DKIM on your domain. Those don't need to be in your SPF record at all. Check each tool's docs for "custom return-path" or "bounce domain".
  • Subdomains per tool (mail., news., support.), each with its own small record.
  • Flatten only the stable part, and keep includes for providers that change often, with a scheduled re-check. See flattening risks.
